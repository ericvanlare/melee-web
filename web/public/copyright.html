What this site serves

The public player serves project HTML, CSS and JavaScript, an Emscripten WebAssembly runtime and its data, audio modules, and a generated renderer pipeline seed. No retail disc image or extracted game archive is served. A disc selected in your browser is processed locally and is not a hosted user submission. The site currently has no user-content area, account service or online upload feature.

How reports are handled

We will review a report about material actually served by this site and may restrict or remove served material while investigating. A report does not control data that remains on a visitor's device after local player use. Do not send confidential material unless the operator requests it.
