Privacy Notice

Updated September 12, 2026. Work in progress.

This notice describes the current public player prototype and the information handled when you visit or use it.

What the player handles

The player loads its HTML, stylesheet, JavaScript modules and same-origin WebAssembly/data files. When you choose a supported ISO, GCM or CISO, bounded reads validate the local disc structure and expected USA 1.02 executable, then copy only the required game data into page-local native memory. The selected file, its operating-system filename, the executable hash, archive names and derived data are handled locally by the current source path.

The current public source path has no application account, analytics, advertising, telemetry or upload feature. It does not send the selected file, filename, executable hash or derived game data to the operator. Gamepad information is read locally to show connected controller descriptions. These statements describe the reviewed source path; deployment verification should confirm the same request and module inventory.

Storage on your device

The player stores keyboard layout and enablement choices in localStorage under melee-prototype-keyboard-v1. If browser storage is unavailable, controls work for the session. The public loader does not use cookies, sessionStorage, IndexedDB, Cache Storage or a service worker. The native renderer may create a page-local pipeline database in its in-memory filesystem; the public loader does not mount IDBFS or persist that database through its cache module.

Audio is disabled in this public alpha. Eject unloads the native scene and reloads the page. That full reload retires the page and WebAssembly heaps, imported archive bytes and decoded assets. It does not clear the keyboard preference, ordinary browser HTTP cache or history, or a graphics-driver cache. The player cannot control those browser and driver caches.

Hosting and network requests

Cloudflare Pages serves the site. Visiting a page necessarily sends network information such as your IP address, requested URL and browser request headers to the hosting provider. The provider may process request, security and operational information to deliver and protect the service. We do not set the provider's operational retention periods, and the absence of application telemetry is not a promise that the provider collects nothing.

Cloudflare describes its practices in its Privacy Policy. The player requests its own same-origin resources; do not put personal information in URLs or choose a disc image for any feature other than the local player. Links to other sites are governed by those sites' practices.

If you contact us

Email goes to the operator and the mail providers involved in delivery. We use your message, sender address and attachments to respond, investigate a reported problem or address a rights claim. We retain correspondence only as needed for those purposes and applicable legal obligations. Do not attach disc images, extracted game data, credentials or unrelated personal information.
