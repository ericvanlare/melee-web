Privacy Notice

Updated September 12, 2026. Work in progress.

This notice applies to the shell preview currently served at WebMelee. This build has no file-selection or disc-processing capability.

Application behavior

The shell downloads its own pages, stylesheet and fullscreen script. It contains no analytics, advertising, telemetry, account, upload or crash-reporting code. It does not read a disc, filename, file hash or derived game asset. The fullscreen control changes the display locally.

Storage on your device

This shell does not create cookies or use localStorage, sessionStorage, IndexedDB, Cache Storage or service workers. Your browser may retain public site files in its ordinary HTTP cache and navigation history. Those files contain no selected disc data. Use your browser's site-data and history controls to clear retained website data.

Hosting and network requests

Cloudflare Pages serves the site. Visiting a page necessarily sends network information such as your IP address, requested URL and browser request headers to Cloudflare. Cloudflare may process request, security and operational information to deliver and protect the service. We have not added application analytics or request logging. This is not a promise that the hosting provider collects nothing.

Cloudflare describes its practices in its Privacy Policy. We do not set Cloudflare's operational retention periods. Avoid including personal information in URLs. Links to other sites are governed by those sites' practices.

If you contact us

Email goes to the operator and the mail providers involved in delivery. We use your message, sender address and attachments to respond, investigate a reported problem or address a rights claim. We retain correspondence only as needed for those purposes and applicable legal obligations. Do not attach game files, extracted assets, account credentials or unrelated personal information.

A future playable release

Disc selection remains disabled. Before enabling it, the project must verify that disc contents and derived game assets remain on the user's device, describe any local cache or preference storage, and update this notice. Claims about a development player do not establish the behavior of a future public build.
