01 / THE PROJECT

Local play is the goal.

An experimental project for bringing Super Smash Bros. Melee to a desktop browser. Accuracy, audio, input and performance verification are ongoing.

02 / YOUR FILES

Your copy. Your device.

This build cannot select or read files. It has no uploads, accounts, advertising or analytics code. Future disc support must keep the selected file and derived game data on your device.

03 / WHAT COMES NEXT

Ready means verified.

Gameplay remains unavailable while the production player, distribution rights and release checks are reviewed. This preview makes no claim of complete or tournament-ready gameplay.

Before disc selection becomes available

You will need to acknowledge: “I am selecting a copy of the game that I am legally authorized to use.” Owning a disc does not automatically authorize every copy or method of access. This project does not provide game files or permission from their rights holders.
